**Name:** Red Glass : Personal Experiments

## Description

Does cutting coffee after 2pm actually fix your sleep? Is making cold brew at home really cheaper? Do morning workouts change your afternoon energy — or do you just like believing they do?

Stop debating. Test it.

Red Glass is a lab notebook for your life. Not a habit tracker — no streaks, no badges, no guilt. You run one small experiment at a time and end it with an answer.

HOW IT WORKS
• Write a hypothesis — "I believe [change] will [effect], measured by [metric]"
• Capture a baseline first, so there's a before-picture
• Switch to the intervention, check in daily with one-tap ratings
• Close with a verdict — supported, refuted, or honestly inconclusive

BUILT TO KEEP YOU HONEST
• Baseline phases and minimum durations resist one-good-day conclusions
• Life happens — log confounders ("was sick Tue–Thu") and contaminated data is flagged and excludable
• Backdated entries are always marked, so past-you can't be quietly edited
• Every experiment must end with a written conclusion in your own words

FAST ENOUGH TO ACTUALLY DO
• Check-ins take seconds: tap a number, tap yes/no, done — with a little celebration when the day is logged
• Reminders arrive exactly when each metric needs measuring, and tap straight into logging
• Ready-made templates: food elimination, routine swaps, make-vs-buy, sleep experiments — or design your own
• Draft experiments with AI: describe your idea, get suggested controls, metrics, and phases

YOURS ALONE
• Everything lives on your device — no account, no cloud, no tracking
• Export your complete data as JSON anytime

Settle your personal debates the way scientists settle theirs: run the experiment. Just test & find out.
